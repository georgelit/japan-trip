The plan, day by day

Saturday 13 February → Monday 22 February 2027

A loop around the Northern Alps with no backtracking: Tokyo, Matsumoto, Hakuba, Okuhida, Takayama, then back through Matsumoto to Tokyo. Matsumoto sits on the way in, which is the trick that makes ten days enough.

Day 1Sat 13 Febdeparture

Fly out of Berlin after 21:00. One stop, roughly 15 hours door to door.

Night: on the plane
Day 2Sun 14 Febarrival

Land at Haneda in the evening. Nothing ambitious: eat something, sleep, let the jet lag start unwinding. Haneda is much closer to the centre than Narita.

Night: Tokyo · book early, this is still Chinese New Year week
Day 3Mon 15 Febtransfer + Matsumoto

Limited Express Azusa from Shinjuku to Matsumoto, 2 h 30, ¥6,620. Matsumoto Castle is a sixteenth century original and a designated National Treasure, and the town does very good soba.

Pick up the car in Matsumoto, drive to Hakuba, about an hour. Settle in.

Night: Hakuba (1 of 4)
Day 4Tue 16 Feb🎿 🏂 Happo-One

Happo-One. 52 km of runs, 21 lifts, 1,071 m of vertical, and the slope the 1998 Olympic downhill was raced on. Ride until early afternoon, then the village.

Worth knowing for Nadir and Rostik: despite its reputation as a skiers' mountain, Happo-One is rated the best resort in Hakuba for snowboarders, precisely because the terrain flows and there are almost no flat sections to skate across.

Rostik has his own programme: the beginner zones are Nakiyama and Sakka at the base, with the school office right there, plus the Shirakaba slope at a maximum of 20°.

Night: Hakuba (2 of 4)
Day 5Wed 17 Feb🎿 🏂 Goryu + 47

Goryu and Hakuba47. These two are physically connected by a gondola at the top and share one ticket: 24 runs, 14 lifts and 2 gondolas across three areas, all inside our Hakuba Valley pass anyway.

Good split for our group. Goryu has the gentle terrain for Rostik, and Hakuba47 has what is widely called the best terrain park in Japan, which makes it Nadir's day.

The pass covers ten resorts, 133 km and 109 lifts, but the valley is 40 km long, so it is one or two resorts a day and no more. The car is what makes even that realistic.

Night: Hakuba (3 of 4)
Day 6Thu 18 Feb🎂 Nadir's day

Last morning on snow, and it is Nadir's birthday, so he picks the mountain. Happo-One again, or north up the valley to Cortina and Tsugaike for trees and deep snow.

Dinner in Hakuba that evening, and the ryokan is the very next night, so the celebration runs across two nights.

Night: Hakuba (4 of 4)
Day 7Fri 19 Febinto the Alps

Drive Hakuba → Matsumoto → Route 158 through the Abo Tunnel to Okuhida, about 2 h 30. This road has never been closed by snow even in deep winter, but it is a narrow pass with a long winding descent, so no hurry.

Afternoon: the Shinhotaka Ropeway. Double-decker gondola to an observation deck at 2,156 m with a 360° view of the Hida range: Yarigatake, Hotaka, Kasagatake, Yakedake. ¥3,300 return. Winter hours are 09:00–16:15, last ascent around 15:30.

This matters more than it sounds. Kamikochi is shut from 16 November to 16 April and the Tateyama Kurobe route does not open until mid-April, so this ropeway is the only way to get close to the Northern Alps in February.

Night: 🏮 Okuhida ryokan with an open-air rotenburo · the one deliberate splurge
Day 8Sat 20 FebTakayama

Okuhida → Takayama, 50 minutes. Sanmachi Suji, the Edo-era merchant quarter, Hida beef, sake breweries.

Afternoon run out to Shirakawa-go and back, 50 minutes each way. The gassho-zukuri village under snow is the picture everybody knows.

Night: Takayama
Day 9Sun 21 Febback to Tokyo

Takayama morning market from 08:00 until around noon, at Miyagawa riverside and in front of Takayama Jinya. Fewer stalls in winter than in the photos, but it is still the best hour of the day here.

On the road by 09:00. Drive to Matsumoto over Route 158 (~2 h 30), drop the car, Azusa to Tokyo, in the city around 15:00. Afternoon and evening in Tokyo, then Haneda for the night flight.

Night: on the plane
Day 10Mon 22 Febhome

Land in Berlin in the morning.

7 nights booked: Tokyo ×1, Hakuba ×4, Okuhida ×1, Takayama ×1

Why exactly these dates

six things to dodge, from two directions

February 2027 starts on a Monday. The dates were not picked for convenience: the first two weeks of February are the worst possible window, hit from both sides at once. Expensive tickets out of Germany, crowded slopes in Japan.

Japan side

DateWhatEffect
6 FebChinese New Year, Year of the Fire GoatWave from China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore. Japanese resorts are their main winter destination. Peak roughly 6–13 Feb
11 Feb (Thu)National Foundation DayMany take the Friday too, so it becomes a four-day weekend on the slopes
23 Feb (Tue)The Emperor's BirthdayLands exactly on a ski Tuesday
Sat & SunJapanese people ski at weekendsMidweek is much quieter for the same money

Germany side

DateWhatEffect
1–6 FebBerlin WinterferienPrice spike on departures from BER
4–10 FebCarnival: Weiberfastnacht 4th, Rosenmontag 8th, Ash Wednesday 10thSchool holidays in several states, another price spike

So 13–22 February is the clean window. We land on the 14th with the Chinese wave gone and the 11th behind us. We ski Tuesday to Thursday, clean midweek days with no holiday anywhere near them. We fly home on the 22nd, the day before the Emperor's Birthday refills everything. And mid-February is the deepest snow base of the season: December and January snow has settled, the spring melt has not started.

One soft spot: 13–14 February is the tail of Chinese New Year week, so the Tokyo hotel for the night of the 14th should be booked early.

Things that will bite if we forget them

checked against official sources

A German International Driving Permit is not valid in Japan. Japan recognises the 1949 Geneva Convention, Germany signed the 1968 Vienna one. German licence holders need an official Japanese translation carried together with the original licence. It comes from ADAC Südbayern, the only licensed office in Europe: €65 in person in Munich, €70 by post, 10–14 working days, and they need the physical licence, not a scan.

Georgi and Nadir already have theirs. Anyone else who wants to drive needs their own.

  • Winter tyres must be booked with the car. Rental companies will not fit them on arrival. Snow tyres or chains are required on Route 158 from mid-November to early April.
  • No car rental exists in Okuhida or Hirayu. Nearest offices are downtown Takayama or downtown Matsumoto, which is why the car is picked up and dropped in Matsumoto.
  • No visa for German passports. Visa-free up to 90 days. JESTA, Japan's ESTA equivalent, only starts in fiscal year 2028, so it does not touch this trip.
  • The Shirakawa-go winter light-up is not available to us. In 2027 it runs on four evenings only, all in January: the 11th, 17th, 24th and 31st, advance booking only. The village itself is open and deep in snow in February, just without the illumination.
  • Day 9 is the tight one. Route 158 on the morning we fly home. The road does not close, but snow slows it. Leaving Takayama by 09:00 leaves about ten hours of buffer.

Still open

these block bookings

NadirToyama: in or out? Nadir wanted a full circle including Toyama, and geographically he is right, it is what makes this a circle rather than a loop. It does not fit in ten days as planned. Putting it back costs an eleventh day, or Shirakawa-go, or a ski day. Toyama is a pleasant modern city: Toyama Bay sushi, the Kengo Kuma glass museum, Kansui Park. Good, not a headline. See the Kanazawa question below, a coastal swap could pull the second half this way anyway.
NadirDoes the birthday shape work? The 18th is the last day on snow, so you pick the mountain, then dinner in Hakuba, then the ryokan the following night. Two nights of celebrating. The alternative is swapping days 6 and 7 so the birthday itself lands on the ryokan with the rotenburo. It does not have to land on a ski day at all, that is only where it sits today. Your call.
EveryoneConfirm the group at four. Working assumption for now. Apartment size, car class and every price on this page depend on it, so it has to be settled before anything gets booked.
RostikSki school, or do we teach him? Affects day 4 only. Worth knowing that a private lesson is priced per instructor rather than per person, so it can cover several of us at once.
EveryoneIs an afternoon plus evening enough for Tokyo? As planned we get roughly 15:00 to 21:00 on day 9. A full Tokyo day needs an eleventh day, or dropping Shirakawa-go. A JDM night drive, see below, would need real time carved out of this same slot.
NadirNagano instead of Matsumoto as the way in? Tokyo → Nagano by shinkansen (77–95 min) then Nagano → Hakuba by bus (~60–70 min) beats the Matsumoto route by roughly 40–70 minutes each way, and Nagano has rental car offices too. Trade-off: Matsumoto Castle would need dropping or re-routing, and it is not yet confirmed a Nagano car naturally passes Matsumoto on the way to Okuhida.
NadirA JDM night drive is missing from the plan. Real operators run chauffeured or self-drive night rides past Daikoku PA, Tokyo's actual expressway car-meet spot, in an R34, GT-R, RX-7, S15 or Supra. Neither current Tokyo slot has room for it yet, day 2 is jet-lagged arrival and day 9 is already tight.
NadirValentine's Day lands on the transit day. 14 Feb 2027 is a Sunday, currently spent landing at Haneda. Shifting to actually spend it at home pushes the return onto the Emperor's Birthday or ski days onto a weekend, the two things these dates were chosen to avoid. Worth knowing Japan's own Feb 14 is not really a date-night occasion either, it is one-directional chocolate-giving with the reciprocal on White Day in March.
NadirThe ryokan night is currently a Friday. Borderline weekend pricing and crowding. Same family of question as the birthday shape, worth reshuffling together if days move.
NadirTakayama or Kanazawa? Swap the old-town pace for Kanazawa's livelier feel and the seafood at Omicho Market, or push further up the coast. Worth flagging Kanazawa's actual reputation runs the other way, it is usually branded "Little Kyoto" for its geisha and samurai quarters. Likely costs the Shinhotaka Ropeway unless it stays a separate stop.
